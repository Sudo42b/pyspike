When firmware emits `funct7=0x00 + rs1==0`, the `mask_funct3=False` (None-key) entry wins by 2-level dispatch precedence and silently NOPs — preserving safety. When firmware emits `funct7=0x00 + rs1!=0`, the dispatcher falls through to the funct3-keyed mm/mm_s/mm_o/mm_v/mm_t handlers.
   
   **CRITICAL routing semantics** (verified against npu.py:125-144 2-level dispatch):
   - The 2-level dispatch in `GtxNpu.custom0` tries `sub_table.get(None)` (sentinel) FIRST.
   - If a None-key handler exists at funct7=0x00, it ALWAYS wins regardless of funct3 → MM never runs.
   - **Therefore the NOP-safety must check `insn.rs1` itself** and re-route to MM by manually invoking the funct3-keyed handler when rs1 != 0.
   - Implementation: NOP-safety reads insn.rs1 (the register INDEX); if 0 → NOP; if !=0 → dispatch to per-funct3 mm handler manually.

    funct7 = insn.funct
